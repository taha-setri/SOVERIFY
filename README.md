# 🛡️ SOVERIFY - Digital Sovereignty & Data Protection Compliance Platform

**Soverify** is an automated compliance and digital sovereignty auditing platform built specifically to align with Moroccan Law **09-08** (CNDP regulations). It provides instant automated audits, comprehensive technical warnings, 30-day remediation plans, and enterprise-grade reporting.

---

## 🚀 Key Features / المميزات الرئيسية

* **🔍 Automated CNDP Audit Engine:** Instant evaluation of target websites against Moroccan Law 09-08 standards, cookie consents, and local data hosting.
* **📊 30-Day Remediation Plans:** Step-by-step actionable roadmaps to bridge compliance gaps and achieve full legal readiness.
* **⚖️ Competitive Benchmarking:** Side-by-side compliance comparisons for organizations and public portals.
* **🚨 Incident Response Guide:** Dedicated frameworks and steps for data breach management under national guidelines.
* **📄 Professional PDF Reporting:** Clean, ISO A4 formatted audit reports ready for export and presentation.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack / التقنيات المستخدمة

* **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Canvas-Confetti
* **Backend:** Node.js (Express), TypeScript, Full REST & Multimodal API
* **AI & Intelligence:** Google Gemini AI (@google/genai) featuring Gemini 3.8 Flash & Deep Reasoning
* **Audit & Compliance Engine:** Moroccan Law 09-08 Legal Database, Sovereign Fallback Engine, CNDP Breach Response & Form Deliberations
* **Database & Cloud:** Firebase Firestore & Local Audit Logs
* **Security & Hardening:** Automated Nginx Hardening Generator, Security Headers & TLS Audit
